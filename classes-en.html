Why TGPU Legacy?

More than a class name. It is a legacy.

TGPU stands for Tok Guru Pulau Ubi, the title by which Ustaz Mamduh’s late grandfather was known and respected for his contribution to knowledge and the community.

This name was chosen so that the teaching of the Quran and Islamic knowledge continues across generations. The same spirit also lives on through Surau Tok Guru Pulau Ubi in the family’s hometown.

Through TGPU Legacy, the aim is simple: to bring learners closer to the Quran through clear, approachable and practical learning suited to each student’s level.

“The best legacy is not merely a name remembered, but knowledge and goodness that continue to benefit others.”

Frequently Asked Questions

Before registering for your first class.

Quick answers for parents and learners who want to understand TGPU Legacy.

My child or I cannot read the Quran at all. Can we join?

Yes. Classes can start from Hijaiyyah letters, basic Iqra’ or the learner’s current level. The trial class helps identify the right starting point.

Are these classes only for children?

No. TGPU Legacy welcomes children, teenagers and adults. Teaching style, pace and materials are adapted to age and level.

Are classes taught in Malay?

Yes. Malay is the main teaching language. Arabic is used when needed for recitation, terminology and Arabic-language learning.

Which subjects can I choose?

Learners may focus on Iqra’ foundations, Quran & Tajwid, Hifz, Arabic, Fardu Ain/Islamic Education or a suitable combination.

Are classes conducted online?

Yes. Main classes are conducted live online using platforms such as Zoom or Google Meet, according to the agreed arrangement.

How does the RM20 trial class work?

The 30-minute session includes a level check, goal discussion and short lesson so the learner or parent can assess suitability before continuing.
