01 / OUR IDENTITY
WHO WE ARE

An ecosystem of ideas made useful.

TGPU is an independent founder-led initiative bringing together learning technology, digital productivity and community-oriented experiences.

We develop and refine websites and applications for practical day-to-day use: from introductory Arabic learning and Hajj/Umrah orientation to family learning and tools supporting small-business workflows.

Each product retains its own identity, audience, technical requirements and release safeguards. The goal is not simply more applications—it is a more helpful and accessible digital ecosystem.

04 / TRUST & RESPONSIBILITY
OUR APPROACH

Credibility comes
from care.

Responsible release, accessible design and truthful communication matter as much as visual polish.

01

Make learning accessible

Design for phones, multilingual audiences and people who benefit from simple, predictable interfaces.

02

Protect users and content

Handle personal data conservatively; require proper content rights and qualified review where relevant.

03

Verify before release

Differentiate prototypes, limited previews and public-ready applications. Do not equate an automated build with real-world approval.

04

Keep public-benefit tools accessible

Do not add fees to applications intended to remain free without founder approval. Commercial development engagements have separate agreed scopes and payment terms.
