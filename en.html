ABOUT TGPU LEGACY

Honouring Knowledge.
Nurturing Generations.

TGPU Legacy carries forward the legacy of Tok Guru Pulau Ubi, Ustaz Mamduh’s late grandfather. Through Quranic guidance, Arabic learning and Islamic education, we nurture knowledge, strong adab and a love of learning for a better tomorrow.

Surau Tok Guru Pulau Ubi in Kampung Telaga Lanas, Pengkalan Kubor, Tumpat, Kelantan carries the family name forward and brings together worship, learning and service.
