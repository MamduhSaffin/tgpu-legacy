Where our name comes from

TGPU stands for Tok Guru Pulau Ubi, the title of founder Muhammad Mamduh Saffin’s late grandfather. Rooted in Tumpat, Kelantan, that family legacy informs our commitment to knowledge, community service and responsible enterprise.
