MALAYSIA CAREERS

Job Portals & a Practical Career Plan

Search for work systematically: verify legitimate opportunities, match your skills to the role, and protect yourself from recruitment scams.

Never pay to get a job. A legitimate employer or recruiter should not ask for your TAC, banking password or an urgent payment through a private message. Verify the sender, company domain and original vacancy before applying.

A practical application plan

  1. Choose three realistic roles: match the stated skills and requirements instead of applying randomly.
  2. Tailor your résumé: highlight experience, projects and skills that directly relate to the role.
  3. Keep a simple tracker: company, role, application date, status and next follow-up date.
  4. Practise a one-minute answer to “Tell me about yourself” using real examples.

General guidance only. Always verify the vacancy and employer before sharing personal documents.
