Business, SME & Selling Online in Malaysia

Starting a small business, registering correctly, finding SME support or selling on marketplaces is easier when you begin with official Malaysian portals and each platform’s official seller-education centre.

Start small, but manage properly. Do not pay a consultant, buy an expensive package or take financing simply because someone promises sales. Understand the business model, costs, registration, tax and platform rules first.

A simple path to start

  1. Identify the customer and test demand before spending heavily.
  2. Calculate the true cost per order, including goods, delivery, advertising, platform commission and returns.
  3. Register when required and keep business money separate from personal spending.
  4. Keep sales, invoice, receipt, refund and advertising records.
  5. Choose one sales channel first, then add channels only when stock, operations and customer service are stable.

Practical

Before taking financing

Calculate instalments, gross margin, cash flow, slow-moving stock and a lower-sales scenario. Financing is capital—not a replacement for a healthy business model.
