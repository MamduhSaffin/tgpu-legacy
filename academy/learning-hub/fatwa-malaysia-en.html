OFFICIAL MALAYSIAN REFERENCES

Malaysia Fatwa: check the authoritative source

This directory brings together official JAKIM and state/Federal Territory mufti links. It is a reference directory, not a new fatwa and not a substitute for advice from the relevant mufti authority.

Before sharing a ruling: fatwa and religious decisions can differ by state jurisdiction and gazettement status. Check the original text, date, scope and whether it applies in your state. For personal or complex cases, contact the relevant state mufti authority.

Before treating an answer as a reference

  1. Prefer the original JAKIM or state mufti link or document.
  2. Check the state, title, date and question context before quoting an answer.
  3. Distinguish explanatory articles, scholarly opinion and a formally gazetted fatwa.
  4. Do not circulate a clipped answer without the original source and context.
