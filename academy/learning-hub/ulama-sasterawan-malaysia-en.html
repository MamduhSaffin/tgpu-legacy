MALAYSIAN KNOWLEDGE & LITERATURE

Ulama, Islamic Thinkers & Malaysian Writers

Malaysia has been shaped by traditions of Islamic learning, Malay language and literature from many backgrounds. Use this page as an introduction to reading works, understanding influence and finding deeper references.

Ulama and Islamic thinkers

Kelantan

Tok Kenali

Haji Muhammad Yusof bin Ahmad, known as Tok Kenali, was an influential figure in Islamic learning and education in Kelantan in the early twentieth century.

Knowledge & education

Syed Muhammad Naquib al-Attas

A Malaysian thinker known for writing on Islam, education, language and the concept of knowledge.

Reform

Sheikh Tahir Jalaluddin

A reformist thinker and educator active in the Malay world and Malaya in the early twentieth century.

Terengganu

Haji Abdul Rahman Limbong

A religious and community figure remembered in the history of popular struggle, land and justice during the colonial period.

Malaysian writers

Poetry & humanity

Usman Awang

A poet and writer whose works often explore humanity, society and national feeling.

Novel & poetry

A. Samad Said

A major literary figure whose writing examines people, language, society and lived experience.

Language

Za’ba

Zainal Abidin Ahmad, or Za’ba, made major contributions to modern Malay language, education and thought.

Literature & society

Shahnon Ahmad

Known for novels and works examining rural society, politics and human questions.

Poetry & scholarship

Muhammad Haji Salleh

A poet and scholar connecting Malay literature with tradition and world reading.

Stories & history

Arena Wati

His novels and writing carry maritime, regional and social experience.

How to explore a figure well

  1. Start with a reliable biographical source and establish dates, places and institutions.
  2. Read the person’s own work where possible rather than relying only on summaries.
  3. Separate historical context from later interpretation.
  4. Keep notes on editions, publication dates and source provenance.

This is an introductory learning page, not a definitive biographical reference.
