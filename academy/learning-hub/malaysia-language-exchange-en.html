A 15-minute daily plan

  1. 5 minutes: review three words from yesterday.
  2. 5 minutes: listen and repeat one short phrase slowly.
  3. 5 minutes: use the new words in a sentence, voice note or short conversation.

Safe and respectful language exchange

Find a study partner

Use a 20-minute weekly session: 10 minutes in one language and 10 minutes in the other.

Keep simple rules

Correct gently, do not pressure anyone to share private contact details, and stop if the conversation becomes uncomfortable.

Use weekly themes

Week 1 greetings; week 2 food; week 3 directions; week 4 school/work. Repeat and use them in real life.
