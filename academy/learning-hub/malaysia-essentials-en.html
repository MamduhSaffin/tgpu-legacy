MALAYSIA · IMPORTANT LINKS

Malaysia Essentials: assistance, health, study & work

A short guide to important public portals. It does not determine eligibility or payment dates—check directly with the relevant agency because programmes, conditions and application periods can change.

Beware of scams. Never share your TAC, password, card number or a “processing payment” through WhatsApp or SMS. Enter services through the official portal.

Before you apply

  1. Read the official portal for eligibility, opening/closing dates and required documents.
  2. Prepare identity, income, bank or student documents only when the official portal genuinely requests them.
  3. Keep reference numbers, status screenshots and confirmation emails.
  4. For problems, contact the agency’s official line or counter—not an unknown person claiming they can “approve” the application.

TGPU Legacy does not operate government assistance programmes and cannot verify or guarantee a user’s eligibility.
