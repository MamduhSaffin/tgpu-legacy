TRAVEL WITHIN MALAYSIA

Malaysia Holiday Destinations by State

Choose a trip based on your mood: beach, nature, history, city, food or family. This is a planning starting point, not a ranking. Check weather, opening hours, access, safety requirements and current prices before travelling.

Plan safely: avoid unsuitable monsoon periods for some islands and beaches, book early during school holidays, and stay away from waterfalls or the sea when weather warnings are issued.

Ideas by state

Kelantan

Kota Bharu, Bachok & the east coast

Malay culture, batik, local food and a relaxed coastal atmosphere.

Terengganu

Islands, Kuala Terengganu & Tasik Kenyir

Sea, islands, fishing communities and nature.

Pahang

Cameron Highlands, Taman Negara & Tioman

Choose highlands, rainforest or island experiences according to weather and budget.

Perlis

Kuala Perlis, Wang Kelian & Gua Kelam

A compact trip for northern food, caves and border-region culture.

Kedah

Langkawi, Alor Setar & Gunung Jerai

Islands, panoramic views, rice fields and northern history.

Penang

George Town, Balik Pulau & Penang Hill

Urban heritage, food, street art and hill scenery.

Perak

Ipoh, Kuala Kangsar & Pangkor

Old-town cafés, limestone caves, royal heritage and islands.

Selangor

Kuala Selangor, Sekinchan & Hulu Langat

Fireflies, rice fields, seafood and nature activities near Kuala Lumpur.

Negeri Sembilan

Port Dickson, Seremban & Jelebu

Beaches, Minangkabau heritage and family breaks close to the city.

Melaka

Historic city, Jonker Walk & Melaka River

Heritage trails, museums, Peranakan culture and evening walks.

Johor

Johor Bahru, Desaru & Mersing

City breaks, beach resorts and gateways to east-coast islands.

Sabah

Kota Kinabalu, Kundasang & Semporna

Mountains, islands and marine experiences—use licensed operators for water activities.

Sarawak

Kuching, Bako & Mulu

Heritage city, national parks, caves and cultural diversity.

Federal Territories

Kuala Lumpur, Putrajaya & Labuan

Cities, architecture, museums, parks and island experiences.

A simple 2-day / 1-night formula

  1. Choose one main focus: nature, food, history or rest.
  2. Keep to a maximum of two nearby areas so the trip is not spent mostly in transit.
  3. Choose accommodation close to the first activity of the following day.
  4. Keep one free activity such as a park, market, beach or heritage walk.

This page is for domestic Malaysia travel only. Weather, closures and access rules can change.
