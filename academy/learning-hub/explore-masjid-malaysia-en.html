Explore Mosques in Malaysia

A mosque is more than a stop for prayer. It is also a place of learning, community, history, architecture and local memory. TGPU helps you begin with official sources and explore with proper etiquette and verification.

Avoid relying on old blog lists for visiting hours, phone numbers or facilities. Start with official systems and recheck before travelling.

Mosque-visit etiquette

  1. Check prayer and visiting times before arriving as a visitor.
  2. Respect mosque clothing and aurah guidelines.
  3. Give worshippers priority and avoid disrupting prayer rows, classes or worship spaces with photography.
  4. Programmes, guided visits and facilities can change; recheck the official source on the day of your visit.
