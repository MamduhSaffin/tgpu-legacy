Student Library & Knowledge

Books and open resources can build a strong learning habit. Choose material appropriate to your level, read a little every day, then explain what you understood in your own words.

A 20-minute student reading routine

  1. 10 minutes: read without switching apps or tabs.
  2. 5 minutes: write three points you understood in your own words.
  3. 3 minutes: note one unfamiliar term and look it up.
  4. 2 minutes: decide what you will read next.

For assignments and research

Start with a narrow question

A focused question is easier to investigate, for example: “How does X affect Y in context Z?”

Evaluate the source

Check the author, organisation, date, evidence and whether the source is primary, academic or opinion.

Do not copy blindly

Paraphrase only after understanding, then cite the source. Use direct quotations only when necessary.
