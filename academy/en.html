Current Educational Contribution

Supporting Islamic education for Muslim children in Europe

TGPU Academy is contributing teaching-development ideas and support in discussions with IEC, an Islamic education initiative involving Muslim children and families in Europe.

The focus is on making classes more structured, interactive and practical while respecting differences in age, language, family background and learning level.

Read the interactive teaching framework →

Structured Topics + Flexible Delivery + More Interaction

A common topic structure gives the programme continuity, while tutors can still adjust language, activities, Arabic/Quran depth and discussion according to their learners.

This describes an ongoing educational contribution and pedagogical discussion, not a claim of a formal institutional partnership.

Why keep Academy as the main name?

TGPU is not limited to Quran classes. This structure allows Quran education, Arabic, Islamic learning and educational content to grow without changing the main identity each time a new programme is added.

Current focus

TGPU Quran is the clearest flagship programme to develop now. The other branches are prepared as a long-term structure and will grow only when the content, teaching resources and needs are clear.

No new schedules, fees or programme promises are added here until they are confirmed.
